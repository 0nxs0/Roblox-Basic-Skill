## Source-grounded context
An NPC that says "Welcome, traveler!" in a chat bubble when you walk up is one of the cheapest ways to give a game personality, explain a mechanic, or point players to the next goal. Roblox already draws chat bubbles for players; `TextChatService:DisplayBubble()` lets you show the same bubble above any part, including an NPC's head.
