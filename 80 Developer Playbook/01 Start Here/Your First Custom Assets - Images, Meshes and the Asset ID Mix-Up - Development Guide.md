## Source-grounded context
Free models get a game started, but your game starts to look like yours when you upload your own logo, button icons, signs and props. Uploading is easy; the beginner traps are elsewhere: images that show in Studio but not from a script, assets that are invisible in the live game because of ownership, and meshes rejected for size.
