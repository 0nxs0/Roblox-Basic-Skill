## Source-grounded context
Roblox games are rarely made alone for long: a builder, a scripter and a UI artist can share one place in real time through Studio's built-in collaboration (still widely called Team Create). It is powerful but easy to misuse: two people editing the same script, a friend with edit rights deleting a map, or a collaborator who cannot join because of the 2026 age rules.
