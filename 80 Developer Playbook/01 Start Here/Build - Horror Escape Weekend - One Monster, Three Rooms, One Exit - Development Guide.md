## Source-grounded context
A tiny horror escape is one of the best weekend projects because fear comes from lighting, sound and timing, not from content volume. Three dark rooms, one item per room, one monster that patrols and chases, and a door that opens when you have everything: that is a full game loop with a start, tension and an ending. This plan keeps the monster brain to about 40 lines and spends the rest of the time on atmosphere....
