## Source-grounded context
Hatching eggs for pets is one of the most popular systems on Roblox, and also one of the most regulated. An egg is a random item: if it can be bought with Robux, directly or through something bought with Robux, Roblox requires you to show every possible outcome and its real odds before the purchase, and to check `PolicyService` for players in regions where paid random items are restricted.
