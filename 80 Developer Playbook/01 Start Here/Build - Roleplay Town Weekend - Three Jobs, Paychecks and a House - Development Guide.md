## Source-grounded context
Roleplay towns look like they need a whole city, but what makes players stay is having a role (cook, cop, doctor), a small reason to act it out, and something to spend money on. Players write the stories themselves; your job is props and places that start conversations. This plan builds a 4-building town, three jobs with one simple task each, a paycheck that does not reward going AFK, and one house you can buy...
**Useful source sections:** The weekend scope, Day 1 - town and jobs (about 5 hours), Day 2 - tasks, a house and safety (about 5 hours).
