## Source-grounded context
"Publish" and "release" are two different steps on Roblox. Publishing uploads your place to the cloud and gives it an ID, but only you and your collaborators can play. Releasing makes it playable by the public, and in 2026 that step has real requirements: an age check, a maturity questionnaire, and extra steps if you want players of all ages.
