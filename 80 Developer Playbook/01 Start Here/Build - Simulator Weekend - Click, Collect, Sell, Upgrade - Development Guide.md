## Source-grounded context
The simulator loop is the most copied loop on Roblox: press to gather, your bag fills, walk to a sell pad, spend coins on a bigger bag or a stronger tool, repeat. It is a great first "real" game because every part is small and server-checked, and the loop is fun with almost no art. The trap is building pets, rebirths, worlds and codes before the base loop feels good. This plan gets the base loop working on day 1...
**Useful source sections:** The weekend scope, Day 1 - the gather loop (about 5 hours), Day 2 - upgrades, saving and juice (about 5 hours).
