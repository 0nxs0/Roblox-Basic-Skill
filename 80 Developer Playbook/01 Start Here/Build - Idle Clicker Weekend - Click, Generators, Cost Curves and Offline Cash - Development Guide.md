## Source-grounded context
An idle clicker is a numbers game in a menu: click for money, buy generators that earn money per second, each copy costs a bit more than the last, and the game keeps paying (a little) while you are away. It is the purest economy exercise on Roblox and needs almost no 3D work, which makes it a good weekend build for someone who wants to practise UI and server-owned data.
