## Source-grounded context
A ClickDetector makes any part clickable: a light switch, a doorbell, a button on a control panel, a collectible you tap. It is the simplest way to react to a player pointing at something in the 3D world, and it already works with mouse, touch and gamepad. This note builds a lamp you click on and off, adds hover feedback, and explains when a ProximityPrompt is the better choice.
