## Source-grounded context
First Obby in 30 Minutes proves you can make 5 stages. A publishable obby needs about 30 stages, a handful of reusable obstacle types, saved progress and a skip button, and it must not lag. The big beginner mistake is putting a script inside every moving part; at 30 stages that is hundreds of scripts.
