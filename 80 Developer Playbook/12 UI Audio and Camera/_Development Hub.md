## Build loop
1. Map UI states to player actions and feedback; test safe areas, keyboard, gamepad, and touch early.
2. Handle focus, latency, respawn, captions, contrast, audio, and camera comfort.
3. Test narrow screens, muted audio, controller navigation, and repeated input.
