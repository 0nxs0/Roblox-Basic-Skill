## Source-grounded context
In November 2016 Thomas Iché (Unity Labs Paris) published a set of Houdini-simulated smoke, fire and explosion image sequences under CC0 1.0. They are plain zip downloads from Unity's site, not Unity Asset Store items, so the Asset Store EULA does not apply. They are among the few free, realistic simulated flipbooks with a clean license.
