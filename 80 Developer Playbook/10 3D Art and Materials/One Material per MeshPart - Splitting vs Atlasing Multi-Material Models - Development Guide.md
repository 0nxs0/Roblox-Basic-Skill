## Source-grounded context
In Blender a single object can carry five material slots: wood, metal, glass, rope, paint. Roblox's texture specification is blunt: "Mesh objects can only have one material assigned." A `MeshPart` takes one `TextureID` or one `SurfaceAppearance` and one Studio `Material`. Models bought online or generated by AI almost always arrive multi-material, so every Roblox artist needs a plan: split into several MeshParts,...
