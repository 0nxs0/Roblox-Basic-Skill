## Source-grounded context
Roblox's genre list has Roleplay & avatar sim: games where players take on roles, often with avatar customization, emphasizing creativity, social interaction and personal expression. Subgenres include Life, Morph Roleplay, Animal Sim, Dress Up and Pet Care. These games have few goals and almost no "win" state, yet they hold huge audiences.
