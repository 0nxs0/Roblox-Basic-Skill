## Source-grounded context
Roblox players browse a sort of game shelf, join, decide within moments whether to stay, and often play in short bursts between other games, on a phone, with friends talking over voice chat. A story built like a novel (slow first act, long exposition, payoff in hour three) is never seen. Mobile narrative designers face the same conditions.
