## Source-grounded context
A large share of Roblox players are children and young teens, many play on phones, and many read the game in a second language through auto-translation. Writing that works for adult PC players (long dialogue, clever wordplay, slow reveals) often fails here: it is skipped, misread, or translated into nonsense. Writing for this audience is not "writing down". It is writing short, concrete and visual, staying inside...
**Useful source sections:** Reading level: short and plain, Show it, then say it, Tone that lands with 9-15 year olds.
