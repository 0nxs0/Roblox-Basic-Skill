## Source-grounded context
Voiced lines make a Roblox story feel expensive, help young and non-English readers, and let players keep their eyes on the monster instead of a text box. They also create a pile of files that must stay matched to text, subtitles and translations through every rewrite. Most small-team VO problems are bookkeeping, not acting: a line edited after recording, a take uploaded twice, a file too loud next to the others....
