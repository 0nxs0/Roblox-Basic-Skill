# Case Study - Patterns Across Hit Roblox Games — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[02 Game Design and Economy/Foundations Planning/Community Knowledge/Case Study - Patterns Across Hit Roblox Games|Case Study - Patterns Across Hit Roblox Games]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
This note pulls together 30 case studies of hit Roblox games and lists what repeats. Use it before writing a design doc: pick the patterns that fit your game, then read the two or three case studies closest to your genre. All page numbers were read on 2026-10-25 from each game's public Roblox page (same data as the public games API); store items are the game passes for sale that day.
**Useful source sections:** The 30 case studies, Pattern 1: one sentence, one object, Pattern 2: other players are the content.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **make fair, measurable player loops**.
- **Define the contract:** For Case Study - Patterns Across Hit Roblox Games, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** Map action, feedback, reward, and next choice; define cost, pacing, and a success measure.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Check exploits, runaway rewards, and no-spend fairness.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Case Study - Patterns Across Hit Roblox Games and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Test first play, repeat play, and edge-case balances.
