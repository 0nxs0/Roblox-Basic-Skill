## Source-grounded context
A Roblox story game lives or dies on what happens after the first ending. Players who finish and find nothing new leave and do not come back; players who see a locked ending, a missing badge or a harder mode replay with friends and bring new players with them. But every extra ending costs writing, art and testing. This note covers how to choose ending structures, how to make them visible with badges, and cheaper...
