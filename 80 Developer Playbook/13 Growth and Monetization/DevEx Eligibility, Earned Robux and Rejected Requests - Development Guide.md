## Source-grounded context
The Developer Exchange (DevEx) is the only legal way to turn Robux into real money. It is a program you qualify for on every request, not a one-time approval, and Roblox reviews how each Robux was earned. Teams that understand the rules before launch avoid frozen cash-outs later.
**Useful source sections:** Eligibility (all required), What counts as Earned Robux, Rates (2026-10-25).

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using DevEx Eligibility, Earned Robux and Rejected Requests and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
