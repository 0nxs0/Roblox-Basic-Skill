# Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Community Discord/Community Knowledge/Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform|Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
Launch day is when every channel you've built fires at once. The usual failures are boring ones: links that point to the wrong place, servers full of players with nothing announced, a trailer with a lyric track rejected from the game page, or the dev busy firefighting while Discord has no information. Use this checklist alongside the technical release checklist. It covers marketing, comms and measurement only.
**Useful source sections:** T-7 days, T-1 day, Launch hour.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Marketing - Launch Day Checklist - Off-Platform and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
