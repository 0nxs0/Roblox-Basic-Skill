# Moderation Appeals for Games, Assets and Accounts — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Publishing Release/Community Knowledge/Policy - Moderation Appeals for Games, Assets and Accounts|Moderation Appeals for Games, Assets and Accounts]]. ## Source-grounded context
Sooner or later an asset, a thumbnail or a whole game gets moderated, sometimes by mistake. Appeals have strict limits (one review, 30 days, owner only), so a rushed or vague appeal can waste the only chance. Treat a moderation hit like a production incident: freeze, gather facts, then appeal once with a clear case.
**Useful source sections:** How moderation happens, The two appeal routes, Hard limits.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Moderation Appeals for Games, Assets and Accounts, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Moderation Appeals for Games, Assets and Accounts and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
