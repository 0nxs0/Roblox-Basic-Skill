## Source-grounded context
Roblox's Acquisition guide suggests partnering "with other creators who make similar games to grow together". It's one of the few off-platform tactics that also creates on-platform traffic, through portals, collab items and shared events. Two small games with overlapping audiences can trade players without paying for ads, and a collab is also a ready-made story for social media ("we teamed up with X"). This note...
