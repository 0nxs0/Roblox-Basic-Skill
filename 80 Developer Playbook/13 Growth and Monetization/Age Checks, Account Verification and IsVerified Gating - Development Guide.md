# Age Checks, Account Verification and IsVerified Gating — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Publishing Release/Community Knowledge/Policy - Age Checks, Account Verification and IsVerified Gating|Age Checks, Account Verification and IsVerified Gating]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
Age checks now sit in front of chat, Team Create collaboration, 16+/18+ games and publishing to younger audiences. As a developer you meet them twice: your own account needs them to unlock creator features, and your players' status changes who can enter and talk in your game. You can also read a player's verification status in code.
**Useful source sections:** What needs an age check (player side), Age groups Roblox uses, What verification unlocks for creators.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Age Checks, Account Verification and IsVerified Gating, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Age Checks, Account Verification and IsVerified Gating and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
