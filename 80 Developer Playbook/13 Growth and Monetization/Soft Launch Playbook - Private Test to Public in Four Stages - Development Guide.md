# Soft Launch Playbook - Private Test to Public in Four Stages — Development Guide

## Source-grounded context
Roblox's discovery gives a new game a short window to prove itself, and community postmortems (see the launch teardowns in Community Resources) keep repeating the same lesson: launch publicly before the first session works, and the first wave of players never returns. A soft launch means getting real players and real numbers in small, controlled steps before you spend on ads or call in YouTubers.
**Useful source sections:** Stage 0: internal (team only), Stage 1: friends and testers (10-50 people), Stage 2: small public (organic only, 1-2 weeks).

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Soft Launch Playbook - Private Test to Public in Four Stages, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Soft Launch Playbook - Private Test to Public in Four Stages and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
