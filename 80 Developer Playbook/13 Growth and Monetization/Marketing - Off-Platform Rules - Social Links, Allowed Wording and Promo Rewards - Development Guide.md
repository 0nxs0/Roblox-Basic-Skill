## Source-grounded context
Marketing outside Roblox works in one direction: TikTok, YouTube, X or Discord send people into the game. Roblox is strict about the other direction. You cannot put a Discord invite on a sign in your lobby, and a "join our Discord for a reward" popup can get the game moderated. Read this before you plan any campaign. It covers the rules that decide which links, words and rewards are allowed.
