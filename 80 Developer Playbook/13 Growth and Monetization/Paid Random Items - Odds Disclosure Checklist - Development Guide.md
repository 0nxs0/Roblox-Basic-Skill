# Paid Random Items - Odds Disclosure Checklist — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Publishing Release/Community Knowledge/Policy - Paid Random Items - Odds Disclosure Checklist|Paid Random Items - Odds Disclosure Checklist]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
Eggs, crates, spins and gacha rolls are some of the best earners on Roblox, and also one of the most regulated. Roblox requires exact odds disclosure for anything random that is paid for directly or indirectly with Robux. This is a release-blocker checklist; run it on every new egg, banner or luck item.
**Useful source sections:** Does the rule apply?, Disclosure format (all must be true), Content rules from the Community Standards.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Paid Random Items - Odds Disclosure Checklist, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Paid Random Items - Odds Disclosure Checklist and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
