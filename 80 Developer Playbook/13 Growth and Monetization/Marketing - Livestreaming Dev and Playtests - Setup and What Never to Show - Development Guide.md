## Source-grounded context
Live streams are where a creator-dev's audience becomes a community. Viewers watch the game take shape, suggest features, and join playtests on the spot. GameDiscoverCo even notes developers streaming gameplay to their store pages as easy-to-watch footage. Streams also carry a risk recorded video doesn't: whatever is on your screen goes out live and unedited, including API keys, webhook URLs, private server links...
**Useful source sections:** 1. Stream formats that work, 2. What never goes on screen, 3. Safe OBS setup for Studio streams.
