## Source-grounded context
Roblox has no UTM parameters. Traffic from outside Roblox lands in the "Other" bucket of the Acquisition dashboard unless it comes through a Share Link. A creator who posts on TikTok, YouTube and Discord with one plain game URL can't tell which channel brought players who stay. One Share Link per channel, per creator and per campaign fixes that. It also makes those players count toward Creator Rewards audience...
