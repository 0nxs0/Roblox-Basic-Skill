## Source-grounded context
Successful games get copied, and games that borrow too freely get taken down. Roblox runs a U.S. DMCA-style notice system for copyright, a parallel process for trademarks, an EU DSA route, and a self-serve Rights Manager for verified rights holders. Knowing both sides lets you protect your game and respond calmly if your own content is hit.
