# Creator Store Distribution and Selling Rules — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Publishing Release/Community Knowledge/Policy - Creator Store Distribution and Selling Rules|Creator Store Distribution and Selling Rules]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
The Creator Store (the old Toolbox marketplace) is where you both take assets for your game and give or sell assets to other developers. Two policy questions matter: what you are allowed to do with a free asset you insert, and what rules apply if you distribute or sell your own models and plugins for USD.
**Useful source sections:** Using other people's Creator Store assets, Distribution requirements, Selling models and plugins for USD.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Creator Store Distribution and Selling Rules, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Creator Store Distribution and Selling Rules and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
