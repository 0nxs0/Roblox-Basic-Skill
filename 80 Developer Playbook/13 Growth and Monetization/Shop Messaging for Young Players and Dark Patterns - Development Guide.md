## Source-grounded context
Roblox's audience is young, and regulators in several regions now look at "dark patterns" (design that pressures or tricks people into spending). The Creator Terms say you must follow all laws on loot boxes, dark patterns and other potentially addictive features. Roblox's guidance is short and practical: make promotions suitable for all ages by default, use PolicyService for restricted features, and drop...
**Useful source sections:** Roblox's own wording guidance, Rules from the Advertising Standards worth copying into your shop, Hard rules (not just style).

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Shop Messaging for Young Players and Dark Patterns, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Shop Messaging for Young Players and Dark Patterns and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
