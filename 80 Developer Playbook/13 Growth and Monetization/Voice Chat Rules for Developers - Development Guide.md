# Voice Chat Rules for Developers — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[13 Growth and Monetization/Publishing Release/Community Knowledge/Policy - Voice Chat Rules for Developers|Voice Chat Rules for Developers]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
Voice makes social and co-op games feel alive, but it is the most age-gated feature on Roblox. You do not control who may speak; Roblox does. What you control is whether voice is on, how it is routed (proximity, team, global), and how your game behaves for the many players who cannot use it.
**Useful source sections:** Who can use voice, Platform limits you must design around, Routing voice with the Audio API.

## Apply it
Use this topic as part of **improve discovery without misleading players**.
- **Define the contract:** For Voice Chat Rules for Developers, write down behavior, inputs, state owner, visible result, and failure response.
- **Build a first slice:** State the audience promise and first-session value; measure one funnel step before offers.
- **Maintain it:** Separate tuning data from behavior, record assumptions, and provide cleanup or rollback before connecting more systems.
- **Project risk:** Show price and benefits clearly, respect platform rules, and avoid coercive timers.

## Validate before shipping
- **Expected path:** Build one small feature using Voice Chat Rules for Developers and record setup and expected result.
- **Boundary case:** Test missing, invalid, repeated, delayed, or interrupted input where relevant.
- **Integration:** Test with neighboring systems and at target device/player scale.
- **Acceptance check:** Compare conversion with satisfaction and test no-spend progression.
