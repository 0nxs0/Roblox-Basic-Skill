## Source-grounded context
A Discord server grows from the outside in. People arrive from your videos, the game page's Social Links (16+ verified viewers only) and creator shout-outs. Whether they stay depends on the first 60 seconds: do they find the right channel, a reason to talk, and something happening this week? This note covers the growth side. Moderation and raid defense have their own notes, linked below.
**Useful source sections:** 1. Turn on Community mode first, 2. Onboarding: route members on day 1 (Discord support article), 3. Weekly reasons to come back.
