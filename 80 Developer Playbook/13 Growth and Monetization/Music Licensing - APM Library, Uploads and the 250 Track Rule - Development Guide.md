## Source-grounded context
Music is the asset type most likely to be pulled automatically: Roblox scans uploaded audio for IP infringement, and copyrighted songs were mass-removed starting in 2018. There are two legal paths for music in your game: Roblox's Licensed Music library, or audio you fully own (or properly license) and upload yourself. Anything else is a takedown waiting to happen.
