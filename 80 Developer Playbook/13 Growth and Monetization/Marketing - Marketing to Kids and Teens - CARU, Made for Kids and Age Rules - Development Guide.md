## Source-grounded context
A large share of Roblox players are children, so your off-platform marketing reaches them too. That brings stricter rules than adult-audience game marketing: children's advertising guidelines in the US, COPPA on YouTube, age limits on social apps, and Roblox's own age gates on links. This note collects what applies to a creator-dev promoting a Roblox game. Not legal advice.
**Useful source sections:** 1. CARU (US children's advertising self-regulation), 2. YouTube "made for kids", 3. Social-app age rules (brief).
