## Source-grounded context
If you pay a YouTuber in Robux, give them free in-game items or an exclusive code, or post about your own game from an account people don't know is yours, that's an endorsement with a material connection. US (FTC) and UK (ASA/CMA) rules require it to be disclosed clearly. The FTC also puts responsibility on the advertiser, meaning you, the developer, to brief and monitor creators.
