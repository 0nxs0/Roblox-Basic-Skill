## Source-grounded context
A "live moment" is something that happens in every server at the same time: a meteor shower at 18:00 UTC, a boss spawn every hour on the hour, a developer-hosted "double luck" hour announced in Discord. These moments create urgency, give players a reason to be online together, and are perfect for Experience Events (players can tap Notify Me and get a notification when it starts).
