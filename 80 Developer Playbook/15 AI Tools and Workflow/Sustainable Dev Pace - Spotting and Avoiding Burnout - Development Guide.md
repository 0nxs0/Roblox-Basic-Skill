## Source-grounded context
Roblox games are often live services: updates every week or two, events, player messages, Discord moderation, analytics. Many developers are teenagers or doing this beside school or a job. That mix is a recipe for burnout, and the DevForum has many threads from developers who lost all motivation mid-project. A game that needs you for two years needs a pace you can keep for two years.
