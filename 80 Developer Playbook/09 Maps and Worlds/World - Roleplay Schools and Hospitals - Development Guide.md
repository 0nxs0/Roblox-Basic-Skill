# World - Roleplay Schools and Hospitals — Development Guide

New practical guidance for [[09 Maps and Worlds/Maps/Community Knowledge/World - Roleplay Schools and Hospitals|World - Roleplay Schools and Hospitals]]. This companion adds a build and validation plan; use the linked source for full reference detail.

## Source-grounded context
In roleplay games, buildings are stages. A school or hospital works when every room implies a role (teacher, nurse, patient, janitor) and gives that role something to sit on, use or open. A beautiful building with nothing to interact with gets abandoned in minutes.
